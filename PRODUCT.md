# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Nevada employers: CFOs, HR and benefits leaders, and business owners who carry the cost of employee health plans. They arrive skeptical and time-poor, having heard "we can lower your healthcare spend" before. Their job to be done is to evaluate whether the ReForm Health Alliance is a credible coalition worth joining, and whether a High Performing Health Plan could actually cut their costs without cutting benefits. Public-sector and provider partners visit too.

## Product Purpose
The ReForm Health Alliance website is the coalition's public face. It exists to make the case for High Performing Health Plans (HPHPs), built on the Health Rosetta model, and to convert credible interest into membership and event attendance. Success is a Nevada employer trusting the alliance enough to join or attend a webinar.

## Brand Personality
Authoritative and evidence-led. Serious, data-backed, institutional, a credible coalition with receipts rather than a vendor with a pitch. Three words: credible, disciplined, resolute. The interface should evoke confidence and competence, not hype.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient blobs, glassmorphism, three identical feature cards, big meaningless metrics.
- Eventbrite / webinar-funnel pages: countdown timers, "seats are limited" pressure, stock photos of people in headsets.
- Stuffy hospital or insurer sites: corporate healthcare blandness, doctors-with-clipboards stock photography, compliance-speak.
- Overdesigned agency sites: scroll-jacking, heavy animation, style over substance.

## Design Principles
- **Show the receipts.** Every claim earns trust with a number, a source, or a named mechanism (transparency, aligned incentives, the Health Rosetta model). Never assert what you can demonstrate.
- **Institutional calm.** Restraint reads as confidence. The navy system, generous whitespace, and steady typographic hierarchy do the persuading; the design never raises its voice.
- **Respect the reader's time.** These are busy executives. Lead with the outcome, make the next action obvious, never bait or pad.
- **Peer, not vendor.** The voice is a coalition of employers who solved this, not a company selling a product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, full keyboard navigation with visible focus-visible states, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation. Audience skews toward older executives, so err toward legible base sizes and generous touch targets.
